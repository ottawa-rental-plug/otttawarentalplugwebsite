Step 5 · Keep the engine running

The playbook

Rules that make this work

  • Commission never touches lifestyle. Every lease bonus goes straight to the current target — debt first, then savings.
  • 3-paycheck months are invisible. Live on two checks every month; the third is a pure payoff bomb.
  • Pay the target the day you're paid. Money that sits in checking gets spent.
  • One win at a time. Snowball order means your first debt dies in month one — momentum is the strategy.

After the emergency fund

The journey doesn't stop at $3,000. Same habit, new targets:

  • Cushion: grow savings to 3 months of essentials (≈ $8,415).
  • Grow: open a TFSA and redirect the monthly surplus into investing.
  • Level up income: at your numbers, each extra lease per month is worth ≈ $1,200/yr — the cheapest raise you'll ever get.
Assumptions: take-home figures, no interest modeled on debts (add a little margin if yours carry high rates — knock out the highest-rate one first if two are close in size), paydays every 14 days from your next payday, commission at 10% of an average lease ≈ $100. Data lives only in this browser.
